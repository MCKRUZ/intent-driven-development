The shape of an engagement

Eight decisions, each signed by a named person, arranged around one continuous build loop. Gates sit on decisions, not on dates — and any signed decision can be reopened from inside the loop, on the record.

KEY a named human signs a machine drafts, checks or reports route back — recorded, risk-tiered

The checking ladder

Every change climbs as far as its risk tier requires. Each rung catches what the one below cannot.

REV A

Nothing auto-advances. A gate says whether you may move on; a named person decides whether you do. Every override carries a name, a reason and an expiry.

Durations are planning figures, not promises — a 4–6 person pod, one medium product; billing milestones map to gates, never to dates. Phases overlap: enablement runs ahead while requirements and design close.
